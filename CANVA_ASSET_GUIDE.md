# Canva Asset Guide

## Purpose
Canva supports visual production; it is not the manuscript source or a required build dependency.

## Approved uses
- Covers and series artwork
- Chapter-opening illustrations
- Concept diagrams and visual learning elements
- Infographics and promotional graphics
- Optional social and launch assets

## Workflow
1. Follow the shared editorial design system and the book's subject accent.
2. Use clear hierarchy, restrained colour, and readable labels.
3. Avoid placing long body text inside image exports.
4. Export approved assets in suitable PNG, JPEG, or SVG formats as appropriate to the asset and renderer.
5. Check resolution, crop, contrast, text legibility, and alternative text.
6. Commit approved exports to the relevant book repository with a short asset note recording purpose, source, and licence/ownership.
7. Keep editable working designs in the designated Canva folder.
